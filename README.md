# WidowFlight (Work In Progress)

WidowFlight is a narrative-driven, boardgame-style strategy game currently in development using the Godot Engine. It blends atmospheric exploration with tactical, wave-based combat, wrapping it all in a dark, supernatural mystery.

## The Premise

Facing imminent bankruptcy, a widow discovers a hidden cabin her late husband owned but kept entirely secret. With no other options, she moves in, only to realize the property is a nexus for monsters, cryptids, and vengeful ghosts. To survive, she must uncover the dark, hidden past her husband left behind and fight back against the entities tied to his secrets.

## Core Gameplay Loop

The game is divided into two primary phases that feed into one another:

* Investigation Phase: Explore the isolated cabin and its surroundings. Search for documents, environmental storytelling, and clues to piece together the husband's unknown past. Discoveries made here provide context and potential advantages for the coming nights.
* Strategy Combat Phase: When the entities attack, the game shifts to a boardgame-style tactical strategy layer. Defend the cabin against escalating waves of cryptids and ghosts, using strategic positioning, resource management, and tactical decision-making to survive until morning.

## Current Development Focus

This project is in active development. I am currently working on:

* Designing enemy wave spawning and AI behaviors for early monster types.
* Building out the initial cabin exploration systems and interactable narrative objects.
* Refining the transition flow between the narrative investigation segments and tactical combat encounters.
